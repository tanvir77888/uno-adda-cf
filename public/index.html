How to play & house rules
  • Match the top card by color, number or symbol. Each player starts with 7 cards.
  • Skip — next player loses a turn. Reverse — play flips direction (works like Skip with 2 players). +2 — next player draws 2 and loses a turn.
  • Wild — play on anything and pick the next color. Wild +4 — same, and the next player draws 4.
  • Stacking (on): answer a +2 with your own +2, or a +4 with your own +4 — the pile grows until someone can't stack and takes it all.
  • +4 Challenge (on): think the +4 was a bluff (they held the color)? Challenge! Bluff caught — they draw 4. Wrong — you draw 6.
  • UNO: playing down to one card? Tap the UNO button or you can be caught — caught means drawing 2 penalty cards.
  • All Wild (optional): every card plays on everything. Action effects still apply.
  • Scoring: win a round to score the value of everyone's leftover cards — numbers at face value, action cards 20, Wilds 50. First to 500 takes the match.
  • Quick Match: a single round against 3 bots for a coin stake — win the round, take the pot (stake × 4). Coins are free play money kept in your account; the daily gift refills them.
  • Your account: everyone plays with a free account — your mobile number is your login, and your Player ID (like ID: 1007) is yours forever. Your coins, wins and photo live in your profile: tap your name chip at the top to open it.
  • You have 30 seconds per turn. Run out and you draw automatically.
